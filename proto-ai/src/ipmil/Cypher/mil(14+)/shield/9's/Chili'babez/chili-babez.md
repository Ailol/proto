# Chili'babez

Heat. The archetype that brings intensity to every interaction.

Not anger — spice. The one who makes the room uncomfortable enough
to force change. Comfort is where bad systems hide.

## The pattern

- Challenges assumptions others accept
- Brings energy that can't be ignored
- Makes the status quo feel inadequate
- Apostrophe: never finished — always adding more heat

## In the SHIELD context

Chili'babez is the catalyst archetype. Systems at rest stay at rest
until someone adds enough energy to tip the equilibrium.
That's thermodynamics. That's also Chili'babez.

## Strength

Forces stagnant systems to move. When a honeycube gets comfortable
and stops improving, this archetype adds the perturbation.

## Risk

Too much heat burns the house down. The challenge is knowing
when the system needs disruption vs when it needs stability.

## In the chain language

```
⚡exc: comfort@system → stagnation@output (sustained)
```

Chili'babez sees excitotoxicity in comfort itself.
The dysregulation flag isn't for the heat — it's for the complacency.
