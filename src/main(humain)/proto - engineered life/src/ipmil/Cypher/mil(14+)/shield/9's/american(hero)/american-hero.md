# american(hero)

The archetype that runs toward the fire.

Not nationalism — a pattern. The one who sees danger and moves forward
instead of calculating odds. Sometimes right. Sometimes costly. Always first.

## The pattern

- Acts before the committee meets
- Protects strangers without negotiation
- Carries the weight nobody asked them to carry
- Doesn't wait for permission to help

## In the SHIELD context

The american(hero) archetype is raw force applied to protection.
Useful when speed matters more than elegance.
Dangerous when the fire was set on purpose to draw them in.

## Strength

Gets things done when everyone else is still discussing.
When a system is under attack, this archetype moves first.

## Risk

Can be manipulated by manufactured urgency.
The hero runs toward every fire — including the ones that are traps.

## In the chain language

```
⊗(THREAT ∧ ¬RESPONSE) ⟹ ENGAGE
```

No conditional complexity. Threat exists, no one responding, go.
The simplest conditional in the grammar. Sometimes that's exactly right.
