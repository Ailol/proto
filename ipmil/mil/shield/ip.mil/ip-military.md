# ip.mil — Intellectual Property: Military

The defense side of IP. What protects the melody.

## What lives here

Protection mechanisms for Cogniotics IP:
- xgrammar EBNF as self-verifying structure (can't fake a parse)
- Formal grammar as proof of originality (random doesn't look like this)
- The folder structure as architectural documentation
- The Wow! signal principle — structured signal is its own proof

## Why military

Not violence. Discipline.
Military protects by being organized, not by being aggressive.
ip.mil is the organizational structure that makes ip.mel defensible.

## Grammar as defense

The GBNF grammar IS the IP defense:
- You can't steal BioChain and pretend you understand it
- The notation self-documents — the structure proves intent
- ::open_ends 0 is verifiable — either the graph closes or it doesn't
- The xgrammar EBNF is the specification — try to fake THAT

## In the open

Open-source the grammar, publish the EBNF, share the notation.
The protection isn't secrecy — it's structure.
Anyone can see it. Not everyone can build it.
